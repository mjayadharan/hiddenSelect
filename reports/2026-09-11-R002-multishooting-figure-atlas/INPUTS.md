# R002 — external inputs

None. Every number, table and figure in this report is produced by the scripts in this folder from the
frozen sources in `deps/` (see `deps/MANIFEST.md` for SHA-256 and commits). No file from R001 or from
`figs/` is read. The three hand-drawn sketches in the theory manuscript
(`Multishooting_and_sparse_optimization.zip`: `shooting_partition.png`, `multiple_shooting.png`,
`comparison_shooting_nodes.png`) were looked at as design references for figures 3–5 and are not inputs.

Result files and their SHA-256 at the time of the final build: `analysis/results/results_hashes.json`
(written by `analysis/make_inventory.py`).
